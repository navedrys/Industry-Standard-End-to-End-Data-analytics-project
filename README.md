# 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer behavior is one of the biggest challenges in retail businesses.  
This project analyzes customer shopping patterns and purchasing behavior to uncover:

✨ What customers buy most  
✨ Which users spend the most  
✨ How discounts affect purchasing decisions  
✨ Which customer groups generate the highest revenue  
✨ What drives customer loyalty and repeat purchases  

The project combines **Data Cleaning, SQL Analysis, and Interactive Dashboarding** to simulate a real-world business analytics workflow.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

A retail company wants to better understand its customers and improve business performance using data-driven decisions.

### The main business questions were:

- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- Do subscriptions increase customer loyalty?
- Which products depend heavily on discounts?
- What shopping behaviors drive repeat purchases?
- How can marketing strategies be optimized?

# 🔍 Key Insights Discovered

## 💳 Subscribers Spend More
Subscription customers generated significantly higher revenue and showed stronger loyalty behavior.

---

## 🚚 Express Shipping Users Spend More
Customers selecting express shipping had a higher average purchase amount.

---

## 🎯 Discounts Influence Buying Decisions
Certain products heavily relied on discounts for higher sales volume.

---

## 👥 Loyal Customers Drive Revenue
A smaller group of loyal customers contributed a major portion of total revenue.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

### ✅ Improve Loyalty Programs
Reward repeat customers with exclusive offers and subscription benefits.

### ✅ Optimize Discount Strategies
Use targeted discounts instead of broad discount campaigns.

### ✅ Focus on High-Value Segments
Prioritize marketing for loyal and subscription customers.

### ✅ Promote Top-Rated Products
Use highly-rated products in promotional campaigns.
